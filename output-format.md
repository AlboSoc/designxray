# Recommended Output Format

## Scene orientation

Two or three sentences describing what is visibly present and any limitations of the image.

## Feature analyses

Use a descriptive heading for each selected feature.

### Feature name

**Observation:** What is directly visible.

**Purpose:** Likely functions.

**Pressures:** Constraints and forces that plausibly shaped it.

**Perils:** Credible failure modes or unintended effects.

**Possibilities:** Redesigns, experiments, transfers, or alternative interpretations.

Repeat for 4–8 features depending on requested depth.

## Cross-cutting deductions

Draw together recurring pressures, dependencies, tensions, and system-level strategies.

## Design thesis

End with one sentence in this form:

> **Design thesis:** This design is fundamentally a negotiation between … and …, achieved mainly through …

The exact wording may vary, but the thesis should be specific to the analysed scene.
